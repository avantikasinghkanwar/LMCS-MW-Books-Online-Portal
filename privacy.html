Privacy Policy

LMCS Books Portal · La Montessori Chain of Schools (Hidimba Educational Society) · Last updated 2 October 2026

The LMCS Books Portal is a classroom tool for teachers. It shows the pages of the LMCS English books on classroom smart panels, with sounds, tracing and activities.

What we collect

  • When a teacher signs in with Google: the Google account's name and email address. We do not receive the Google password and cannot see anything else in the account.
  • Class activity records: which book page was opened, for how long, and how many touches, tracing strokes, sound taps and game answers happened on it. These are counted per class and per teacher.
  • Teacher details: the campus and class the teacher teaches.

What we do not collect

  • No student names, photos, voices or any personal information about children.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.

How it is used

To show school leaders which classes have reached which book page, how the portal is being used, and to keep the teacher list accurate. Records are kept in a Google Sheet owned by La Montessori Chain of Schools and are not sold or shared with anyone outside the school organisation.

Who can see it

Only the school administrators of La Montessori Chain of Schools.

Contact and deletion

To ask for a teacher's details to be corrected or removed, contact avantika.hazri@gmail.com.
